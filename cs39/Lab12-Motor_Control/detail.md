### Lab 12 - Motor 

For this lab we will be doing experiment #10 from the experiment guide. Setup the board and components as described. Note that the yellow, green, and white wires in the circuit are not required for this lab and are not installed. The sequence of the program is as follows:

Drive forward at full speed
* Wait 1 second
* Stop the motor
* Wait 1 second
* Change direction to reverse
* Wait 1 second
* Stop again
* Wait 1 second
* Loop back to the beginning
Summit your project as an archive along with a video showing the program execution. Place a piece of tape on the motor shaft so it is easy to see the motor change direction.
