# Lab Extra Credit
This lab is for extra credit.

The lab requires the motor controller, the potentiometer, and a push button The connection for the motor controller is the same as the motor lab, along with the same connections from previous labs for the potentiometer and push botton. The requirements for this lab are:

* The motor is continuously operational
* When the potentiometer is turned the motor should increase and decrease speed
* When the push button is pressed the motor should reverse direction until you release the button
* When going in reverse or forward the potentiometer will affect the motor speed.
Note that because of the power draw of the motor when the potentiometer decreases the speed of the motor may drop off earlier than expected.

Summit your project as an archive along with a video showing the program execution.
